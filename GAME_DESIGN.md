# EVERPENDRA — Mission 01

## Mission
Raat Ka Raaz

Enter an old Indian-inspired haveli at night, collect a relic, avoid the guard and escape.

## Core loop
Explore → observe patrol → hide/distract → collect relic → escape.

## First playable slice
1. Player
2. Camera
3. Small haveli
4. One guard
5. Vision cone
6. One hiding spot
7. One relic
8. Exit
9. Win/lose UI
10. Mobile joystick/buttons

## Art direction
Original Indian-inspired fantasy adventure. Use original assets and fictional locations.
